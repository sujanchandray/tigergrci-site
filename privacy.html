Privacy policy

This policy explains how personal information is handled on the TigerGRCi founder website and Community. It covers this marketing site only. It is not the privacy notice for a self-hosted TigerGRCi product installation.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who we are

TigerGRCi is built by Sujan Chandra Ray. For privacy questions about this site, contact sujanray@ymail.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us, plus limited technical data needed to run the site:

  • Community and contact forms. Message content, optional name, optional email, rating, topic choices, and related fields you enter.
  • Work-with-me or enquiry details. Name, email, organisation context, and the goals you describe when you reach out.
  • Browser drafts. Unsent Community drafts may stay in your browser storage until you submit or clear them.
  • Technical logs. Standard hosting and form-service logs such as IP address, browser type, and time of request, used for security and reliability.

We do not ask for payment card details on this site. We do not intentionally collect special-category data through Community.

How we use information

  • To receive suggestions, compliments, and product reviews, and to improve TigerGRCi
  • To reply when you leave an email address or ask for a conversation
  • To moderate and publish Community wall posts that are approved on purpose
  • To protect the site against abuse, spam, and security incidents
  • To keep basic records of what was submitted and when

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Community submissions to build advertising profiles.

Where submissions go

Form submissions are delivered to the founder inbox through a form service (currently Formspree). That provider processes the message so it can reach the inbox. Hosting for this static site may also process requests needed to serve pages. Those providers act under their own privacy terms for the services they run.

Community wall

Public wall posts are moderated. Nothing appears on the wall until it is reviewed and published on purpose. If you submit a review or compliment for publication, do not include secrets, credentials, or other people’s personal data. You may ask us to remove a published wall item that identifies you by emailing sujanray@ymail.com.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses local browser storage for Community draft convenience. Hosting or analytics tools, if enabled later, may set cookies needed for security or basic usage measurement. You can clear site data in your browser at any time.

Sharing

We share personal information only when needed to:

  • Operate the form inbox and host the site
  • Comply with law or respond to a lawful request
  • Protect people, the site, or our rights against abuse or serious risk

Retention

Inbox messages are kept as long as they remain useful for product improvement or follow-up, then deleted or archived in the ordinary course of work. Browser drafts remain until you clear them. Published wall items stay until removed in moderation or at a reasonable request.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to the founder inbox. No method of transmission or storage is perfect. Please do not send passwords, private keys, or highly sensitive evidence through Community forms.

Your choices

  • Submit without an email if you prefer not to be contacted
  • Ask us to delete or correct information you sent, where we still hold it
  • Ask us to take down a wall post that identifies you
  • Clear drafts and site data in your browser

Email sujanray@ymail.com with “Privacy” in the subject line.

Children

This site is aimed at professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

International visitors

TigerGRCi is Bangladesh-first. Information you submit may be processed in countries where the founder or service providers operate. If that is not acceptable for your organisation, contact us before submitting.

Product installations

When you run TigerGRCi in your own environment, your organisation controls that data. This founder-site policy does not replace your internal privacy notice or your contracts with users of a deployed instance.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site or Community evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means the revised policy applies to new activity.
